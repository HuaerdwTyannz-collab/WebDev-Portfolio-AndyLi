Accessibility

My website uses clear headings and short paragraphs. This makes the content easier to read.

The navigation is simple, so users can move between pages easily.

I added alt text for images. This helps screen readers understand what the images show.

I used a simple color scheme with strong contrast. This makes the text easier to see.
